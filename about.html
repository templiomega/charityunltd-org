CHARITY UNLIMITED

Larger Than the Individual

ABOUT

Charity is an enduring phenomenon expressed through voluntary exchange.

Charity is circulation — at all levels. 🖤

We hold that human beings participate in something Greater than their mortal selves alone through acts of fairly and regularly Giving to one another of their own accords:

Whether understood as an literal collective consciousness; an archetypal intelligence; an unseen metaphysical order between people; the Emergent Life of human efforts in tandem; another aspect of mental reality not presently understood; or simply the consequences of our interdependence, we call this Greater relation that breathes hope into our systems "Charity" as Deity.

Charity is not required to be an object of literal worship, but the conceivable living archetype of free circulation, exchange, generosity, and consequential change, akin to how certain ancient Platonists might have seen Good in today's world:

Charity becomes visible wherever one person freely gives and another freely receives, both without expectation.

Our ministry exists to serve it: to encourage generosity; to preserve dignity in receiving; and to keep what is entrusted to others moving honestly between human hands.

We do not require exact agreement about what lies behind Charity except that it is self-evident in the Lives it touches.

Our Order simply supposes that its nature may be spiritual, emergent, otherworldly, symbolic, and/or otherwise, but the Fullness of its Grace is beyond our [present] understanding:

The Exchange between souls is of Greatest Import at every level of the human experience; and we believe its practice is Enough.

Give Freely, Receive Freely, and Keep Charity Honest.
